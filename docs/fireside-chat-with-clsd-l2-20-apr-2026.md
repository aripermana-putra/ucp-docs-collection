## Fireside Chat -  Universal Control Plane (UCP)

## 1\. Background

Universal Control Plane(UCP) is a newly formed team, established around the end of 2025.

The team is working toward a unified platform that can provision, manage, and monitor services across Rakuten cloud environments, including both public and private cloud.

Because UCP is effectively a control plane integrating with other existing control planes and platform services, its success depends not only on technical execution, but also on:

- alignment across teams
- prioritization from leadership
- support from service owners
- integration with existing internal platforms

This is especially relevant for private cloud, where UCP may require custom Crossplane providers and service-specific integrations for compute, Kubernetes, database, storage, and other internal platforms.

## 2\. Purpose of This Session

This fireside chat is intended to create an open discussion with Yamasaki-san to better understand leadership perspective on UCP, especially in the following areas:

- strategic importance of UCP within CLSD
- near-term expectations for the team
- definition of success for Q2 and FY26
- release prioritization and scope control
- onboarding and adoption strategy
- cross-team collaboration and leadership support

## 6\. Prepared Discussion Questions

### 6.1 Strategic importance and near-term success

How important is UCP within CLSD’s priorities this year, and what is the single most important outcome you would like to see from the team by the end of Q2 2026?

Possible reference points:

- PoC completion
- MVP scope freeze
- first tenant onboarding

### 6.2 Scope and first release priorities

Given the current UCP direction and feature set, which capabilities are truly mandatory for the first release, and which ones should be intentionally deferred to later phases?

### 6.3 First onboarding target

Which tenant, internal service provider, or use case should UCP prioritize first for onboarding, and why do you think that is the right first target?

### 6.4 Delivery speed vs operational safety

How should UCP balance speed and innovation with the division goal of zero S-rank incidents, especially as a new control plane integrating with multiple existing systems?

### 6.5 Expected business impact

What measurable impact do you most want UCP to create this year?

Possible reference points:

- better developer experience
- lower operational cost
- faster provisioning
- stronger multi-cloud standardization
- other leadership priorities

### 6.x UCP & OneCloud Quality Concerns

UCP is intended to provide a unified experience, but its effectiveness is constrained by the inconsistencies and quality gaps in the underlying OneCloud services. How do you see us balancing the role of UCP: should it be a thin orchestration layer that exposes these limitations, or an opinionated platform that sets a higher quality bar for all OneCloud providers?

### 6.y Self-Service Maturity in CLSD

We’ve discussed the need to accelerate self-service maturity within CLSD to improve our velocity. From your perspective, what are the primary 'blockers' preventing teams from reaching that next level of automation, and how can we better support teams in prioritizing this shift over short-term manual fixes?

### 6.z Engineering Best Practices & Quality Commitment

We’ve seen that schedule pressure often leads to bypassing critical engineering practices, such as CI/CD and automated testing, creating technical debt that eventually slows us down. How can we work together to redefine our 'Definition of Done' or project planning cycles to ensure that engineering excellence is treated as a non-negotiable requirement for delivery, rather than an optional layer to be added later?
