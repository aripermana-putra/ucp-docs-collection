**What you need:** Textbook Ch.2, notebook

  Read the dialogue aloud 3× until the pronunciation flows naturally. Don't rush.

- [ ] **[20 min] Vocabulary — study and write**
  Go through the Ch.2 vocabulary list. For each word:
  1. Say it aloud
  2. Write the kana 2× in your notebook

- [ ] **[10 min] Vocabulary — self-test**
  Cover the kana column. Go through each word and try to produce the Japanese from meaning alone.
  Write any you missed 3×. Then reverse: cover meanings, produce English from kana.

## Sessions 10–16: Katakana

Katakana is used for: foreign loanwords, foreign names, onomatopoeia, emphasis.
Each katakana character has the same sound as its hiragana equivalent.

### Katakana Reference

| | a | i | u | e | o |
|--|---|---|---|---|---|
| — | ア | イ | ウ | エ | オ |
